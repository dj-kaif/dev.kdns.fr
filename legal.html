Terms & Privacy

The plain-language version of how this site works, what happens with subdomains, and what little data is involved.

Terms of use

dev.kdns.fr is a free personal project, not a company or a paid service. A few things follow from that:

  • Subdomains are given out at my sole discretion. There's no guarantee any project gets one, and no guarantee of uptime once it does.
  • A subdomain can be taken back at any time, for any reason — most commonly if a project breaks the law, breaks Katabum's rules, or turns out to be something other than what was described.
  • Whoever holds a subdomain is responsible for what they put on it. I don't review, host, or take responsibility for the content of other people's projects beyond deciding whether to grant or revoke the subdomain.
  • Everything here is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind.

Privacy

This site doesn't have forms, cookies, accounts, or analytics — there's nothing here to collect your data with.

If you email or DM me, that conversation happens through your email provider or Discord, under their privacy policies, not mine. My host may log basic request data, like IP address, as routine server behavior — that's standard for any website and not something this project adds on top of.

No cost, no catch

This is free. I don't charge for a subdomain, run ads on this site, or make any money from it. If that ever changes, this page will say so.
