Present Your Project

Got a project?

Building something cool? A tool, web app, open-source project, community project, or something else that deserves a place on the web?

You can present your project to me. If I like the idea, I may provide you with a subdomain under dev.kdns.fr for your project.

yourproject.dev.kdns.fr app.dev.kdns.fr docs.dev.kdns.fr

This isn't a giveaway of the domain itself — just an invitation to host something worth sharing under it. There's no guarantee every project gets a subdomain, but every idea gets a real look.

What I'm looking for

A few things that would make good use of a corner of this domain:

Useful developer tools

CLIs, libraries, extensions — anything that makes another developer's day easier.

Open-source projects

Code worth sharing, worth forking, worth building on together.

Experimental projects

Half-finished ideas and weird prototypes welcome. Especially those.

Community projects

Tools, bots, or spaces built for a group of people to use together.

Personal projects

Something you built for yourself that turned out worth showing off.

Interesting web applications

Small apps that do one thing and do it well.

Creative ideas

Art, generators, oddities — anything that doesn't fit a neat category.

Got something unusual? Even better — that's usually the interesting stuff.
